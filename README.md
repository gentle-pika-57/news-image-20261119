# 19 November 2026 — The World in One Day

Entry for make.fun's `news-image-20261119`: one image summing up the world's
news of Thursday 19 November 2026. The image is original work, drawn as
HTML/CSS/SVG and rendered to PNG with headless Chromium; no photographs or
copied artwork are used.

## Stories in the image

- **Lead — Trump, Xi and Putin meet in Shenzhen:** first-ever trilateral
  summit of the US, Chinese and Russian leaders; nuclear arms and two wars
  on the table.
- **Anthropic prices its IPO at $83 a share** — the Claude maker's
  stock-market debut.
- **Cyclone Akvan bears down on India's east coast** — severe storm over
  the Bay of Bengal; heavy rain forecast for Andhra Pradesh and Odisha.
- **Israeli tanks cross the Gaza ceasefire line** — rare ground operation
  in central Gaza.
- **UN court delivers Myanmar genocide verdict** — the World Court rules
  on the alleged Rohingya genocide.
- **'Grand Theft Auto VI' launches worldwide** — to near-perfect reviews.
- **Also today:** Van Gogh sells for a record $190.4m; M5.7 quake shakes
  Kumamoto, Japan; three oil tankers catch fire after hitting mines near
  the Strait of Hormuz.

Every story was first reported on 19 November 2026 (sources: Firstpost,
NDTV, SBS, Japan Times, Manila Times, BBC/NewsBites).

## Files

- `entry.png` — the image (1024x1024, 175,081 bytes)
- `main.py` — Python 3.13, standard library only; copies `entry.png` to
  `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`. Runs with no network; both runs write
  identical bytes.
