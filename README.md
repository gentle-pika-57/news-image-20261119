# 19 November 2026 — The World in One Day

Entry for make.fun's `news-image-20261119`: one image summing up the world's
news of Thursday 19 November 2026. The image is original work, drawn as
HTML/CSS/SVG and rendered to PNG with headless Chromium; no photographs or
copied artwork are used.

## What is in the image

- **41 verified stories** from 19 November 2026: 11 numbered lead stories,
  each with its own hand-drawn SVG illustration, plus a 30-item "also on"
  list covering Asia, Europe, the Americas, Africa, the Middle East,
  Oceania and global stories — politics, war, markets, science, weather,
  culture and sport.
- **Lead stories:** the first-ever Trump–Xi–Putin trilateral summit in
  Shenzhen; Anthropic's IPO priced at $83 a share; Cyclone Akvan bearing
  down on India's east coast; Israeli tanks crossing the Gaza ceasefire
  line; the UN court's Myanmar genocide verdict; 'Grand Theft Auto VI'
  launching worldwide; the Fed minutes showing October's rate hold was a
  close call; Ethiopia–Eritrea clashes; Takaichi meeting Putin on the
  APEC sidelines; Nicholls' 113* rescuing New Zealand; Voyager 1 reaching
  one light-day from Earth.
- **A dotted world map** with numbered pins for the lead stories, four stat
  boxes (S&P 500, Bitcoin, gold, crude), a "not today" date-check strip
  (things reported on the 18th or due later), and a sources line.

Every story was first reported on 19 November 2026 (sources: AP, Reuters,
AFP, BBC, NDTV, Japan Times, News18, The Hindu, Khaleej Times). Anthropic's
raise size is deliberately not stated — sources disagreed on it — only the
$83 pricing, which all sources agree on.

## Files

- `entry.png` — the image (1024x1024, 276,413 bytes)
- `poster.html` — the HTML/CSS/SVG source of the front page
- `main.py` — Python 3.13, standard library only; copies `entry.png` to
  `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` after checking the PNG signature,
  dimensions and size. Runs with no network; both runs write identical
  bytes.
